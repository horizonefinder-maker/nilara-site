# Research notes: irupu-falls-from-wayanad

Scheduled: 2027-01-13. Facts checked: 18 Nov 2026. "Search excerpt" = read in the search result text, not a full page fetch.

## Facts used and sources
- Onmanorama (published 10 Dec 2020; URL dated 2018) read 18 Nov 2026: near Kutta, close to Gonikoppal, Kodagu; Brahmagiri range on Wayanad border; 8 km from Kutta; ~700 m walk; maintained by Karnataka tourism dept; Rs 50/head; changing rooms; bathe within railing; swells in heavy rain; Kutta entry to Nagarhole; Tholpetty not far.
- Kerala Tourism Tholpetty newsletter: Tholpetty 20 km NE of Mananthavady on Kodagu road. Kerala Tourism Thirunelli page: Thirunelli reachable from Kutta.
- Outlook Traveller (search excerpt): Irpu is an entry gate to Brahmagiri Wildlife Sanctuary.
- mysore.ind.in ?p=1433 (search excerpt): Kutta → 2 km border → 17 km Kattikulam → 10 km Mananthavady.
