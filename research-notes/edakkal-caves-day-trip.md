# Research notes: edakkal-caves-day-trip

Scheduled: 2026-12-23. Facts checked: 18 Nov 2026. "Search excerpt" = read in the search result text, not a full page fetch.

## Facts used and sources
- Kerala Tourism Wayanad page (read 18 Nov 2026): Ambukuthi hill, Sulthan Bathery area; ~16 km from Sulthan Bathery; ~45 min trek; ticket office near top; cleft 22 ft x 96 ft x 30+ ft; three sets of petroglyphs, some ~7000 years; only known Stone Age carvings in South India; discovered 1895 by Fred Fawcett; carving types; iron railing.
- Kerala Tourism Malabar page (search excerpt): 12 km from Sulthan Bathery.
- Kerala Tourism newsletter Jun 2021 (search excerpt): ~1,200 m above sea level, 45-min trek.
- Onmanorama 3 Jan 2026 (read 18 Nov 2026): cap 1,920/day by Archaeology Dept; entry from 8:30 am, none to hilltop after 4 pm; closed Mondays; plastic-free; Rs 50 Indian, Rs 60 foreign, still cam 25, video 100, jeep shuttle 70; cap often reached by afternoon; limits since 2018 floods, not relaxed.
