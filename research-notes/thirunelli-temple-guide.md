Scheduled: 2026-12-09. Facts checked: 18 Nov 2026. "Search excerpt" = read in the search result text, not a full page fetch.

## Facts used and sources
- Kerala Tourism Thirunelli page (read 18 Nov 2026): ~30 km from Mananthavady; base of Brahmagiri; Kashi of the South; Vishnu (Chaturbhuja); legends; 30 granite pieces; no well, stone aqueducts; Vilakkumaadam incomplete on east; Panchatheertham with Vishnupada; faces east; route via Mysore road, turn at Kattikulam, forest; also from Kutta; temple guest house and small lodges; >1,000 years believed; copper plates of Bhaskara Ravi Varma I & II; Unniyachi Champu.
- Kerala Tourism Papanasini photo page: ~1 km from temple; ancestral rites performed here.
- Kerala Tourism Brahmagiri trekking page: trek starts from Forest Dept camp shed at Thirunelli.
